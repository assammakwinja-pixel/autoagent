# AI Tech Stack: The Engine of ZARAHA

## 1. Image Generation (Hyper-Realism & Consistency)
*   **Primary:** **Midjourney (v6+)**. Unmatched for cinematic lighting and high-fashion aesthetics. Use "Character Reference" (`--cref`) to maintain ZARAHA's face across all prompts.
*   **Secondary:** **Stable Diffusion (Flux.1 / SDXL)** with custom LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) trained on ZARAHA's specific face for precise control over poses and outfits.
*   **Upscaling:** **Magnific AI**. Essential for that "Luxury" high-res finish that makes the skin look hyper-realistic.

---

## 2. Video Generation (Cinematic Motion)
*   **Primary:** **Runway Gen-3 Alpha** or **Luma Dream Machine**. For high-quality, physics-accurate cinematic shots.
*   **Secondary:** **Kling AI** or **Sora (when available)** for longer, more complex storytelling clips.
*   **Animation/Motion:** **Pika Labs** or **Leonardo AI Motion**. Great for subtle movements like hair blowing or eye blinking.

---

## 3. Voice Cloning & Audio
*   **Voice:** **ElevenLabs**. Use the "Professional Voice Cloning" feature to create ZARAHA's signature "Velvet Mid-Atlantic African" tone.
*   **Speech-to-Speech:** ElevenLabs or **MetaVoice**. To inject human emotion and rhythm into the AI voice.
*   **Music:** **Suno** or **Udio**. For custom "African Futuristic" background scores (Afro-beats meets Synth-wave).

---

## 4. Lip Sync & Avatar Animation
*   **Primary:** **HeyGen** or **Synthesia**. Best for educational or "talking head" content with perfect lip-sync.
*   **Advanced:** **Live2D** or **Unreal Engine 5 (Metahuman)**. For live-streaming and high-end cinematic interaction.
*   **Lip Sync Post-Production:** **SyncLabs**. To sync audio to any video footage (even cinematic Runway clips).

---

## 5. Social Media Automation & Scripting
*   **Scripting:** **ChatGPT (GPT-4o)** or **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**. Using a custom "ZARAHA Sovereign" prompt profile.
*   **Automation:** **Make.com** or **Zapier**. Connecting OpenAI to social media schedulers.
*   **Scheduling:** **Metricool** or **Later**. For managing the 6-platform distribution strategy.

---

## 6. Editing & Cinematic Visuals
*   **Primary Editor:** **CapCut (Desktop)**. High-speed, mobile-friendly, and has excellent AI plugins.
*   **Professional:** **DaVinci Resolve**. For high-end color grading to achieve the "Luxury African" look.
*   **HUD/VFX:** **Adobe After Effects**. For the "Future-HUD" overlays and holographic effects.

---

## 7. Recommended Mobile-Friendly Workflow
1.  **Generate Image:** Midjourney (via Discord mobile).
2.  **Animate Image:** Runway App or Luma.
3.  **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs (mobile site).
4.  **Lip Sync:** HeyGen mobile.
5.  **Edit & Captions:** CapCut.
6.  **Post:** Native Social Apps.
